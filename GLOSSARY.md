# FounderOps Planning

FounderOps planning connects strategic goals to outcomes and the work needed to achieve them.

## Language

**Initiative**:
An outcome brief within an Epic that defines the goal, success criteria, and scope for related Deliverables.

**Deliverable**:
A concrete, reviewable outcome under an Initiative. Its brief defines the expected result, acceptance criteria, and binding constraints, leaving implementation decisions to the Owner.

**Sub-Issue**:
A discrete work step under a Deliverable. It may describe the practical steps needed to carry out the work.

**Prompt Template**:
Writing guidance for AI-assisted planning. When templates apply, a draft uses the general template and the template for its item type.

**Prompt Template Audience**:
The FounderOps profiles to which the active Prompt Templates apply, either all eligible profiles or a selected set.
