## Measurement method and results

[Matched measurements](credential-verification-measurements.md) are complete: 32 HTTP states × 30 samples per version, 30 controlled mixed-snapshot races per version, 30 calendar and 30 private production-HTTPS browser interactions per version, three actual Next process starts per route/version, and five separate module/deadline scenarios × 30 samples per version. All 32 matched HTTP response digests agree. Historical development-HTTP browser values are separate from the matched production-HTTPS browser series. Every warm row has 30 serialized samples; median, nearest-rank 95th percentile and maximum include outliers. Process-cold startup/first-response and controlled module expiry/renewal series are separate.

Auth, Directory, evidence-store, permit, accounting, application data and provider work are counted at actual transport invocation. A warm membership hit does not make the full action network-free. Proxy and original route/authority function-body timings are measured directly; parallel request durations are not added to claim wall time. Compilation/module loading and response/browser completion have explicit separate boundaries.

The old 11.39-second production delay was not reproduced. Local synthetic zero-delay provider measurements cannot establish its cause, predict hosted latency or guarantee an improvement.
